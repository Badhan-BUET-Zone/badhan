# 5. The screen and the menu

> **Written for readers with no coding experience.** This manual assumes no programming or
> technical knowledge of any kind — only that you use the Badhan app.

## The bar at the top

Across the top of every screen there is a coloured bar with three things on it:

- **Three horizontal lines**, top left. Tap them to open and close the menu.
- **The Badhan logo and name**, in the middle.
- **Three vertical dots**, top right. This holds **Sign Out**.

On a phone the bar shrinks out of the way as you scroll down, and comes back when you scroll
up.

## The menu

Tap the three lines. A panel slides in from the left. At the very top it shows **your name**
and, underneath it, **your level** — Volunteer, Hall Admin or Super Admin. That is a quick way
to check what you are allowed to do.

Below that is the list of pages. **You only see the entries your level allows**, so your menu
may be shorter than a colleague's.

| **Feedback** | Messages and registrations sent from the public pages. [Chapter 20](20-donor-feedback.md) | Volunteer and above |
| **Donor Creation** | Opens into three entries below | Volunteer and above |

| **Public Contacts** | The emergency contact list. [Chapter 13](13-public-contacts.md) | Volunteer and above |

At the bottom of the menu is a button that reads **Activate Night Mode** — or **Activate Day
Mode** if you are already in the dark one. It switches the app between light and dark
colours. Your choice is remembered on that device. The same switch is also on the My Profile
page.

## Two pages with no menu entry

**Emergency contacts without signing in.** The public contact list can be opened by anyone,
signed in or not, and shared with people outside Badhan. See
[chapter 13](13-public-contacts.md).

**A donor's profile.** You do not reach a profile from the menu; you reach it by searching
and tapping **See profile** on a donor's card.

## Messages the app shows you

**A coloured strip along the bottom.** Green means it worked — *"Donation inserted
successfully"*. Red means it did not. These fade away by themselves; you do not have to
dismiss them.

**A thin bar at the very top.** Something is loading. Wait for it to finish before tapping
again.

**A box in the middle asking you to confirm.** Anything that cannot be undone easily —
deleting a donor, deleting a donation, signing out — asks first. Read it before you tap
**Confirm**; there is no undo afterwards.

**A coloured badge in a corner naming the version.** If you can see one, you are on a test
copy of the app, not the real one. Nothing you do there affects real records — and nothing
you do there is saved for real either.

## Sharing a link to what you are looking at

Several pages have a **share** icon that copies a link to whatever is on your screen — a
donor's profile, or a set of search results. Paste it into a message to a colleague. When
they open it they will be asked to sign in first if they are not already, and then land
exactly where you were.
