# 9. Call records

> **Written for readers with no coding experience.** This manual assumes no programming or
> technical knowledge of any kind — only that you use the Badhan app.

## The problem this solves

An urgent request goes out. Four volunteers open the app, and all four search for O− donors in
the same hall. All four see the same three names at the top. All four start dialling.

That donor's phone rings four times in ten minutes, from four different people, about the same
request. It is annoying for them and wasteful for you.

Call records fix this. When you call a donor through the app, everyone else can see that the
call was made.

## Logging a call

You do not log a call by hand. **Use the Direct call button** on the donor's card, and the app
does two things at once:

1. hands the number to your phone's dialler, so the call starts
2. writes a call record with your name and the current time

You will see *"Added call record"* at the bottom of the screen.

If you ring the donor from your own contacts instead of through the app, no record is made and
nobody else knows you called. That is the one habit worth building: **call from the app.**

## What everybody else sees

On the expanded search card, two lines:

- **Last called** — the date and time of the most recent call, or *Unknown*
- **Called N times in last 3 days**

Look at that number before you dial. If it says 2 or 3, this donor has already been asked
recently. Try the next name on the list first.

## The full history

The donor's profile has a **Call History** section. Tap **Show N calls** and you get every
logged call, with:

- who made it
- their hall
- their level
- the date and time

If a donor complains they are being called too often, this is where you check whether that is
true, and who has been calling.

## Deleting a call record

Each entry has a delete button. Deleting asks you to confirm first.

Delete a record when it is simply wrong — a misdial, or a call logged against the wrong donor.
Do not delete records to hide that a donor was contacted; the count is what stops the next
volunteer calling them again.

## Who can do this

Anyone who can see the donor can log a call and can delete a call record — volunteers, hall
admins and super admins alike. As everywhere else, a donor of another hall is out of reach
unless they are marked **Public Data**.
