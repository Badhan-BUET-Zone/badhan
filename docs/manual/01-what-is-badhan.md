# 1. What Badhan is

> **Written for readers with no coding experience.** This manual assumes no programming or
> technical knowledge of any kind — only that you use the Badhan app.

[← Back to contents](README.md) · [Next: Getting the app →](02-getting-the-app.md)

---

Badhan is a voluntary blood donors' organisation. It was founded on 24 October 1997 and works
across the country. Badhan, BUET Zone was inaugurated on 18 September 2005 and covers seven
hall units at BUET: Ahsanullah, Chatri, Nazrul, Suhrawardy, Sher-e-Bangla, Rashid and
Titumir.

The Badhan app exists for one reason: **so that when someone needs blood, a volunteer can
find a suitable donor in seconds instead of asking around.**

## What the app holds

The app is a shared record book. Two kinds of things are written in it.

**Donors.** A donor is one person: their name, phone number, blood group, which hall they
belong to, their student ID, their room, and a few notes. Every person in the app is a donor,
including the volunteers themselves.

**Donations.** Each time a donor gives blood, someone writes the date into that donor's
record. The app then counts the donations by itself and works out when that donor will be
ready to give again.

Everything else in the app — the search, the reports, the call history — is built on top of
those two things.

## How a blood request actually gets handled

This is the whole app in six steps. The rest of the manual is the detail.

1. Someone needs blood. You learn the blood group they need.
2. You open the app and **search** for donors of that blood group in your hall
   ([chapter 6](06-searching-for-donors.md)).
3. The app shows you who is **available** — meaning enough time has passed since their last
   donation that they can safely give again.
4. You **call** one of them straight from the app, and the app quietly notes that you called
   ([chapter 9](09-call-records.md)). The next volunteer can then see that this person has
   already been contacted, so nobody gets four calls in one afternoon.
5. If the donor agrees and gives blood, you **write the donation date** into their record
   ([chapter 8](08-recording-donations.md)).
6. That donor now counts as unavailable for the next four months, so nobody calls them again
   too soon, and their donation shows up in the organisation's reports.

## What the app is not

- **It does not call or message donors on its own.** Every call is made by a person. The app
  only helps you find who to call and remembers that you called.
- **It is not open to the public.** Only Badhan members can sign in. The one page anyone can
  see without signing in is the emergency contact list
  ([chapter 13](13-public-contacts.md)).
- **It does not decide who is allowed to donate medically.** The four-month rest period is a
  guideline the app displays; the judgement is still yours and the donor's.
