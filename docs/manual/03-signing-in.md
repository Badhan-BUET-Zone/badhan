# 3. Signing in

> **Written for readers with no coding experience.** This manual assumes no programming or
> technical knowledge of any kind — only that you use the Badhan app.

## There is no sign-up button

You cannot create your own Badhan account. Accounts are given out, not requested from inside
the app.

**Only Badhan members can sign in.** Being in the app as a donor is not enough — a donor is
just a record in the book, and a record cannot log in. Someone must first make you a
volunteer, and only then can you be given a password. That step is explained in
[chapter 12](12-members-and-promotions.md).

If you have no account and think you should, ask your hall admin.

## Signing in

The sign-in screen has two boxes:

- **Phone** — your 11-digit phone number, the one Badhan has on record for you. Type it the
  normal way, starting `01`.
- **Password** — at least 4 characters. Tap the eye icon at the right of the box if you want
  to see what you are typing.

Then tap **Sign In**.

If either the phone number or the password is wrong, the app says **"Incorrect phone /
password"**. It deliberately does not tell you which of the two was wrong.

**After three failed attempts you must wait five minutes** before trying again. This is not a
fault; it is there so nobody can sit and guess at passwords. Wait it out — the message goes
away on its own.

## Getting your first password

A new volunteer has no password yet. Someone above you — your hall admin, or a super admin —
generates a **recovery link** for you from your donor profile and sends it to you (see
[chapter 7](07-the-donor-profile.md)).

Opening that link takes you to a page that greets you by name and asks for a new password:

- at least **6 characters**
- typed twice, and the two must match

Tap **Save**, and you are signed in with your new password.

Use the same process if you have forgotten your password. There is no "forgot password" link
on the sign-in screen — ask a hall admin or super admin for a fresh recovery link instead.

> **The link is single-use and short-lived.** If you leave it sitting in a chat for days and
> then open it, it may no longer work. Just ask for a new one.

## Staying signed in

You stay signed in on that device until you sign out. Closing the app or the browser does not
sign you out.

If you open a link into the app while signed out — for example a link to a donor's profile
that a colleague sent you — you will land on the sign-in screen, and after signing in the app
takes you straight to the page you were trying to reach.

## The guest login

Under the Sign In button is a small link: **Or, login as guest**.

This signs you into a **demonstration version** of the app. Everything looks and behaves
normally, but:

- every donor you see is made up
- nothing you change is saved anywhere
- some buttons, such as generating a recovery link, are switched off

Use it to learn your way around, or to show someone else how the app works, without any risk
of touching real records. Nothing you do as a guest can affect real donors.

## Signing out

Tap the **three dots** at the top right of the screen and choose **Sign Out**. The app asks
*"Sign out?"* to make sure you meant it. This signs out the device you are holding, and no
other.

To sign out everywhere at once — because you lost a phone, or signed in on someone else's
device and cannot get back to it — go to **My Profile** and use **Signout from all devices**
([chapter 14](14-my-profile-and-devices.md)).
