# 19. Glossary

> **Written for readers with no coding experience.** This manual assumes no programming or
> technical knowledge of any kind — only that you use the Badhan app.

[← Previous: When something goes wrong](18-when-something-goes-wrong.md) · [Contents](README.md)

---

Every term this manual uses, in one line each.

**Archived.** A record deliberately set aside. It keeps everything it had, but is left out of
every ordinary search. Only a super admin can archive or unarchive.
[Chapter 15](15-statistics-and-reports.md)

**Attached.** A hall setting for students not attached to any of the seven halls. Members of
every hall can reach these records.

**Available.** A donor whose last blood donation was more than 120 days ago **and** whose last
platelet donation was more than 12 days ago. Shown as a green badge.

**Available / Not Available filters.** The two tick boxes on the search page. At least one must
be ticked.

**Badge / chip.** A small coloured oval on screen carrying one fact — a level, a blood group,
a count.

**Batch.** The first two digits of a student ID; the year a student started. Search results are
grouped by batch.

**Bookmark.** A flag saying "this donor is worth remembering". Visible to everyone, with your
name attached. Formerly called *Active Donor*. [Chapter 10](10-bookmarked-donors.md)

**Call record.** A note that somebody rang a donor, made automatically when you use **Direct
call**. [Chapter 9](09-call-records.md)

**Certificate.** A printable page thanking a donor, carrying their name, their student ID and a
QR code. Opened from the donor's profile, downloaded as a PDF and printed. Anyone can open a
certificate without signing in, which is why it shows nothing but the name and the student ID.
[Chapter 7](07-the-donor-profile.md)

**Comment.** A free-text note on a donor, readable by everyone who can see that donor.

**CSV.** A plain table file, the kind Excel and Google Sheets produce with *Save as CSV*. Used
for uploading many donors at once. [Chapter 11](11-adding-new-donors.md)

**Database (DB).** The record book itself — the single store where every donor, donation, call
and comment lives. The word appears on the Backup & Restore buttons.
[Chapter 16](16-super-admin-tools.md)

**Department.** Worked out from the third and fourth digits of a student ID. Never typed in
separately.

**Designation.** The app's word for your level: Donor, Volunteer, Hall Admin or Super Admin.
This manual usually says **level**. [Chapter 4](04-roles-and-permissions.md)

**Direct call.** The button that dials a donor and logs the call at the same time.

**Donation.** One occasion of giving blood, recorded as a date. Blood and platelet donations
are counted separately. [Chapter 8](08-recording-donations.md)

**Donor.** A person whose details are in the app. Also the lowest level — and a level that
cannot sign in.

**Guest login.** A demonstration sign-in with made-up donors, where nothing is saved. Safe for
learning and showing. [Chapter 3](03-signing-in.md)

**Hall.** The residence hall a donor belongs to: Ahsanullah, Chatri, Nazrul, Rashid,
Sher-e-Bangla, Suhrawardy, Titumir, plus **Attached** and **(Unknown)**.

**Hall Admin.** The member who runs one hall's records. One per hall.
[Chapter 12](12-members-and-promotions.md)

**Level.** How much of the app someone can use. See *Designation*.

**Logs.** The app's record of every action taken by every person. Super admins can read them.
[Chapter 15](15-statistics-and-reports.md)

**Member.** Anybody at volunteer level or above — that is, anybody who can sign in.

**Platelet donation.** A donation of platelets rather than whole blood. The rest period is 12
days instead of 120.

**Public Contacts.** The emergency contact list anyone can read without signing in.
[Chapter 13](13-public-contacts.md)

**Public Data.** A tick on a donor that makes them reachable by members of every hall, not just
their own. [Chapter 7](07-the-donor-profile.md)

**Password recovery link.** A one-time, short-lived link that lets a member set a password. It
signs that person out of every device the moment it is generated.
[Chapter 3](03-signing-in.md)

**Promote / demote.** Moving somebody up or down a level. Only hall admins and super admins can
do it. [Chapter 12](12-members-and-promotions.md)

**QR code.** The square black-and-white pattern on a certificate. Pointing a phone camera at it
opens that same certificate on Badhan's website, so the paper can be checked against the screen.
[Chapter 7](07-the-donor-profile.md)

**Rest period.** The time after a donation before a donor is shown as available again: 120 days
for blood, 12 days for platelets. It affects display and filtering only — the app never refuses
a date because of it.

**Student ID.** The 7-digit number identifying a BUET student. Batch, then department, then the
individual number.

**Super Admin.** A member with no hall limit, who can do everything including reports, archiving
and backups.

**(Unknown).** A hall setting meaning nobody recorded which hall. These records can be edited by
members of any hall, so that whoever finds out can fix it.

**Volunteer.** A working member of Badhan. Can search, call, record donations and add donors,
within their own hall. Cannot promote anybody.
