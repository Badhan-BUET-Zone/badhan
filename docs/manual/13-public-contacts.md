# 13. Public contacts

> **Written for readers with no coding experience.** This manual assumes no programming or
> technical knowledge of any kind — only that you use the Badhan app.

## What this page is

**Public Contacts** is the one page in the app that **anyone can open without signing in**. It
is the emergency number list: a patient's family finds it, sees who to ring for their blood
group, and rings them.

The page is written in Bangla, because the people reading it are not Badhan members. It
welcomes the visitor and tells them to contact the members listed below for urgent blood
donation.

You can share the page freely — with a hospital, in a Facebook group, anywhere. There is a
**Share** button at the bottom that copies the link.

## How it is organised

Contacts are grouped by blood group. Under each heading — *for A+ blood*, *for B+ blood*, and
so on — sits a card per member with their **name**, their **phone number** and a **Direct
Call** button that dials straight away.

There are five headings in all:

**A+**, **B+**, **O+**, **AB+**, and one that covers **any negative blood group**. Negative
groups are rare enough that they are handled by one shared set of contacts rather than four
separate ones.

## Publishing someone to the list

*Super admins only.*

You do not add contacts on the Public Contacts page itself. You do it from the person's
profile:

1. Open the member's profile ([chapter 7](07-the-donor-profile.md)).
2. Scroll to the **Public Contacts** box on the right — it only appears for super admins.
3. Choose a blood group from the dropdown: A+, B+, O+, AB+ or All Negative.
4. Tap **Publish**.

That member's name and phone number are now on a page the whole world can read.

The same box lists whatever this person is already published under, as small buttons. Tap one
to remove that entry. If they are not published at all, it says *"This contact is not
published for the public to see."*

## Before you publish anybody

**Ask them first.** Publishing puts a real person's phone number on the open internet, where
strangers will ring it at three in the morning. Nobody should find out they are on the list by
being called.

**Publish members, not ordinary donors.** The list is for people who have taken on the job of
fielding emergency calls.

**Keep it current.** When someone leaves Badhan, remove them from this list before you demote
or archive them. A published number that nobody answers is worse than no number.

**The blood group here is the group they can help with, not their own.** These are
coordinators, not necessarily donors of that group.

## A note on the delete button

If you are signed in, you will see a **Delete** button beside each contact on the Public
Contacts page. **It only works for super admins.** If you are a volunteer or hall admin and
tap it, the app will refuse. Nothing breaks — but the button being there is not permission.
