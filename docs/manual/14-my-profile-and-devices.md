# 14. My profile and devices

---

**My Profile** is your own record, plus a few settings that belong to you alone.

## Your own details

The top of the page is exactly the donor profile described in
[chapter 7](07-the-donor-profile.md) — your name, phone, blood group, hall, room, address and
comment. You can edit your own details even where you could not edit somebody else's.

Your own donations are here too. If you gave blood and nobody recorded it, you can record it
yourself.

There is a **share** button beside your name that copies a link to your profile, for sending
to a colleague who needs your details.

## Changing your password

In the **Settings** section of your own profile you get two boxes, **New Password** and
**Confirm Password**. Type the same thing in both — at least 6 characters — and tap **Save**.

If they do not match, the app says *"Password does not match"* and will not save.

You do not need your old password to do this, because you are already signed in. If you have
*forgotten* your password and cannot sign in at all, you cannot use this page — ask a hall
admin or super admin for a recovery link instead ([chapter 3](03-signing-in.md)).

## Settings

**Switch to dark theme.** Turns the app's colours dark. Remembered on this device only; your
phone and your laptop can differ.

**Enable archive search.** *Super admins only.* Turns on searching for archived donors. It
switches itself off after 24 hours, and the hint under it tells you roughly how long is left —
*"Automatically turns off in about 19 hours"*. More in
[chapter 15](15-statistics-and-reports.md).

## Your signed-in devices

Every device you have ever signed in on stays signed in until you do something about it. This
section shows them all.

Tap **Get recent logins**. The page then shows:

**Current Device** — the one in your hand, with its browser, device name, operating system and
network address.

**Other Devices** — every other place you are signed in, each with the same details and a
**delete** button. If this is the only device, it says *"This is the only logged in device for
your account."*

### Signing out one device

Find it in the list, tap its delete button. That device is signed out; yours is untouched.

Do this the moment you realise you signed in on a friend's phone, a lab computer, or a device
you no longer have.

### Signing out everything

**Signout from all devices** signs out every device including the one you are using, and
returns you to the sign-in screen. Use it if you have lost a phone, or if you suspect somebody
knows your password. Change your password afterwards.

> A **password recovery link** also signs you out everywhere as soon as it is generated. If
> you are suddenly signed out on all your devices, somebody has probably just made a recovery
> link for your account — check with your hall admin.

### Reading the device list

The entries name browsers and operating systems, so they may not look familiar. Judge them by
how many there are and whether the count matches your habits. Four entries when you only ever
use one phone is worth asking about. When in doubt, sign out of everything and sign back in —
you lose nothing by doing so.

## Delete Account

There is a **Delete Account** button on this page. **It does not work yet.** Tapping it just
says *"Account deletion is still under construction"*. Nothing is deleted. If you genuinely
need your record removed, ask a hall admin or super admin
([chapter 7](07-the-donor-profile.md)).
