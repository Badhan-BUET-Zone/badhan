# 10. Bookmarked donors

[← Previous: Call records](09-call-records.md) · [Contents](README.md) · [Next: Adding new donors →](11-adding-new-donors.md)

---

A bookmark is a flag you put on a donor to say **"this one is worth remembering"** — someone
reliable, someone who answers the phone, someone who has said to call them any time.

Bookmarked donors get their own page in the menu, so you can go straight to a short list of
good names instead of running a fresh search.

> This feature used to be called **Active Donors**. It is the same thing under a clearer name.

## Bookmarking someone

Open the donor's profile. Beside their name is a **bookmark icon**. Tap it, and a small panel
opens with a switch labelled **Bookmarked donor**. Turn it on.

That is all. The panel also shows **Marked by:** and a name, so you can see who bookmarked
this donor — it may not have been you.

Turn the switch off to remove the bookmark.

## The Bookmarked Donors page

Open **Bookmarked Donors** from the menu. You get the same donor cards as a search result,
with the same expand, call and add-donation buttons.

Two controls sit at the top:

- **Filters** — opens the same filter box as the search page, so you can narrow the bookmarked
  list by blood group, batch and so on.
- **Show donors marked by me** — tick this to see only the donors *you* bookmarked. Leave it
  unticked to see everyone's.
- **Reload** — fetches the list again.

## Bookmarks are shared, not private

This is the part people get wrong. **A bookmark is visible to everybody**, not just to you.
When you bookmark a donor:

- a bookmark icon appears beside their name in everyone's search results
- they appear on everyone's Bookmarked Donors page
- your name shows as the person who marked them

That is deliberate — it is a recommendation to your colleagues, not a private note. If you
want to leave a note only about how to handle this donor, use the **comment**
([chapter 7](07-the-donor-profile.md)) instead.

## Small things worth knowing

- A donor can only be bookmarked **once**. If it is already bookmarked, the app says *"Active
  donor already created"* — someone got there before you.
- Bookmarking follows the usual hall rule: you can bookmark donors of your own hall, and
  donors marked **Public Data** from any hall.
- Bookmarking has nothing to do with availability. A bookmarked donor who gave blood last week
  is still resting, and the card will say so.
- **Archived donors keep showing on this page** even though they are hidden from searches. If
  you see an orange **Archived** tag on a bookmarked donor, that is why.
